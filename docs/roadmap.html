    Read the grid

    ARLO finds every slot in your open stash and recognizes the icon in each one, without you hovering. Your question about recognition settled the approach: "OpenCV sounds like the frontier option for this work." Answer: OpenCV for the geometry, a learned model for icon identity, OCR stays for text. changed 11 Oct

    Will contain

    • A fingerprint baseline first, expected to fail on lookalikes, which is the evidence for the model
    • Slot lattice from edges and contours, frame stillness check
    • An embedding model over a gallery of the item icons, plus crops learned from your hovering
    • Tier numerals on icons by glyph matching, stack counts by OCR

    ARLO knows what you own

    One slow scroll through the stash teaches ARLO your inventory. Your words: "Maybe when my hotkey opens the inventory, you start watching and updating a local database of my inventory?" Local only, nothing leaves your machine.

    Will contain

    • An "unsure" flag on a category when the numbers stop adding up

    It reads your progress too

    Open a workshop or project page once and ARLO reads which tiers are done, so "keep 3" turns into "keep 1 more". Your words: "You might need to know how the project pages are laid out and infer what progress is made per project."

    Will contain

    • Screenshots of your workshop and project pages at your resolution, from you
    • A reader for the workshop overview and station pages
    • A reader for project pages and the quest list

Calls that are yours to make

  • Which recycle row does the game show? One hover on a Power Bank settles the chip label.
  • Expeditions 1 to 4 are over. Show them, hide them, or hide anything ended by default? Hidden by default until you say.
  • Some project phases ask for coins, not items. Skip them, or show "fund 50,000 ₡" as a line?
  • One optional package. Installing gtk4-layer-shell lets the overlay experiment try the Wayland-native mode too.
  • Three lines in .env.example, which agents cannot touch: TOOLTIP_CHIPS=true, CAPTURE_LIBRARY=false, CAPTURE_LIBRARY_DIR=debug/captures.
