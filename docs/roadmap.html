Calls that are yours to make

  • Expeditions 1 to 4 are over. Show them, hide them, or hide anything ended by default? Hidden by default until you say.
  • Some project phases ask for coins, not items. Skip them, or show "fund 50,000 ₡" as a line?
  • One optional package. Installing gtk4-layer-shell lets the overlay try the Wayland-native mode too. Only needed if X11 ever fails.
  • Blueprint find locations. MetaForge's terms ask for attribution and want paid projects to ask first. ARLO is free. Use their vote data with a credit in the README, or stick to the wiki table alone?
  • Banjo's price. It sells for 2,000 in ARLO and 1,000 in ARC Tracker. Check in game.
  • 7 recycle rows differ between ARLO and ARC Tracker, mostly Sheet Metal from furniture. Check in game.
  • Recycle rows where the APIs and the wiki disagree. Anvil I to IV and Arpeggio I to IV look swapped, plus Wasp Driver and Synthesized Fuel. Check in game.
  • Four catalog names appear twice ignoring case (Colorful Shoes and three others). The database keeps the last row. Leave it, or dedupe in the updater?
  • A few lines in .env.example, which agents cannot touch: TOOLTIP_CHIPS, CAPTURE_LIBRARY_DIR, OCR_ENGINE, ADVICE_CARD, STASH_BADGES and SHOW_STATUS_WINDOW. The old capture switch in your own .env can go, it is ignored now.
